Privacy Policy — Mom's Suitcase (Annemin Bavulu)

Developer: Velmoon Games · Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Information we collect

You do not need an account to play. We do not collect your name, email address or location, and we do not run our own servers. Your game progress (levels, stamps, items and settings) is stored on your device.

2. Google Play Games (cloud save and achievements)

If Google Play Games is set up on your device, the game signs in to it automatically. Your game progress is then also saved to your Google account using Google Play Games Saved Games, so you can restore it on a new device, and the achievements you earn are shown in Google Play Games. This data is stored by Google, not by us. Google Play Games may process your player ID and profile name. You can play without Google Play Games. You can delete your Play Games data, including saved progress and achievements, from the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.

3. Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your advertising ID, device and operating system information, IP address and ad interactions to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. With your consent, this data may also be used for personalized ads. See how Google uses data in ads.

4. Notifications and reviews

Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device only and can be turned off in the game settings. The game may show Google Play's in-app review window; we do not receive your rating or review content through it.

5. Your choices

Players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland see Google's consent form and can change their choice later in the game settings. You can turn off personalized ads or reset your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings › Google › Ads).

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

7. Data deletion

Uninstalling the game deletes the data stored on your device. The copy of your progress in Google Play Games is not deleted by uninstalling; you can delete it, together with your achievements and advertising data held by Google, through the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings. You can also email us for help.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
