Privacy Policy — Mom's Suitcase (Annemin Bavulu)

Developer: Velmoon Games · Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Information we collect

You do not need an account to play. We do not collect your name, email address or location, and we do not run our own servers. Your game progress (levels, stamps, items and settings) is stored on your device.

2. Cloud save and achievements

Android — Google Play Games.

If Google Play Games is set up on your device, the game signs in to it automatically. Your game progress is then also saved to your Google account using Google Play Games Saved Games, so you can restore it on a new device, and the achievements you earn are shown in Google Play Games. This data is stored by Google, not by us. Google Play Games may process your player ID and profile name. You can play without Google Play Games. You can delete your Play Games data, including saved progress and achievements, from the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.

iPhone and iPad — iCloud and Game Center. If you are signed in to iCloud, a copy of your game progress is saved to your iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage), so you can restore it after reinstalling or on a new device. If you are signed in to Game Center, the achievements you earn are shown in Game Center, which may process your Game Center player ID and nickname. This data is stored by Apple, not by us; we cannot access the copy in your iCloud account. You can play without iCloud or Game Center: iCloud saving works only while you are signed in to iCloud, and you can sign out of Game Center in your device settings.

3. Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your advertising ID or other device identifiers, device and operating system information, your IP address and the approximate (coarse) location derived from it, ad interactions, and app performance and crash data, to show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. With your consent, this data may also be used for personalized ads. On iPhone and iPad, the game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency) before your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) can be used; if you do not allow it, you will still see ads, but the advertising identifier will not be used. See how Google uses data in ads.

4. Notifications and reviews

Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device only and can be turned off in the game settings. The game may show the in-app review window of Google Play or the App Store; we do not receive your rating or review content through it.

5. Your choices

Players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland see Google's consent form and can change their choice later in the game settings. On Android, you can turn off personalized ads or reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads (the menu name may vary by device). On iPhone and iPad, you can change the game's tracking permission at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

7. Data deletion

Uninstalling the game deletes the data stored on your device. The copy of your progress in Google Play Games is not deleted by uninstalling; you can delete it, together with your achievements and advertising data held by Google, through the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings. On iPhone and iPad, the copy of your progress in iCloud is also not deleted by uninstalling; it stays in your Apple Account. Game Center achievements are managed by Apple in your Game Center account. You can also email us for help.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
