Bavul Panel – Terms of Use

Velmoon Games · Last updated: 25 November 2026

1. About

Bavul Panel is an internal tool of Velmoon Games for managing the statistics and video posts of our own game accounts. It runs only on our own computer and is used only by Velmoon Games.

2. YouTube Terms of Service

Bavul Panel uses YouTube API Services. By using Bavul Panel, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

3. Privacy

Use of Bavul Panel is subject to the Bavul Panel Privacy Policy, which also references the Google Privacy Policy. Users must accept the privacy policy before connecting a YouTube account.

4. Posting content

  • Videos are uploaded only to the account owner's own channel and only after the owner approves each post.
  • The owner sets the title, description and visibility (public, unlisted or private) of every upload.
  • Only content that the owner has the rights to may be uploaded.

5. Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time with "Disconnect" in the panel or at Google security settings.
