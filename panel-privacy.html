Bavul Panel – Privacy Policy

Velmoon Games · Last updated: 25 November 2026

What Bavul Panel is

Bavul Panel is an internal tool used only by Velmoon Games to manage the promotion of our own game, Mom's Suitcase (Annemin Bavulu). It runs only on our own computer. It is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else, and it has no other users.

YouTube API Services

Bavul Panel uses YouTube API Services. By using Bavul Panel you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we access

After the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, Bavul Panel accesses the following data of our own YouTube channel only:

  • Channel name and statistics (subscribers, total views, video count)
  • Our uploaded videos and their statistics (views, likes, comments count)
  • YouTube Analytics reports for our channel (daily views, likes, shares, average view percentage)
  • Comments posted on our videos (to read and reply to our audience)
  • Uploading videos that we choose, with the title, description, language versions and visibility (public, unlisted or private) that we set. Every upload is approved by us before it is sent.

The panel also shows statistics from our own AdMob, Google Play, App Store, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It does not access data about other people's channels or accounts.

How the information is used and stored

  • The data is used only to show our own statistics and to publish our own videos.
  • All data, including sign-in tokens, is stored only on our own computer in a local database. It is not uploaded to any server of ours.
  • The data is not shared with any internal or external party, not sold, and not used for advertising.
  • The panel refreshes the data regularly and checks on each refresh that its access is still authorized.
  • Bavul Panel does not show third-party content or advertisements, and does not use cookies. It stores one display preference (the selected date range) in the browser's local storage on our computer.

Deleting data and revoking access

  • Selecting "Disconnect" for YouTube in the panel immediately deletes the sign-in token and all YouTube data stored by the panel.
  • Access can also be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revocation, stored YouTube data is deleted on the next refresh and within 30 days at the latest.
